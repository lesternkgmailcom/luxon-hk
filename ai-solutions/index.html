If you quote ocean freight, this sounds familiar

Most forwarders we meet lose hours every day to the same three problems.

Rate sheets in every format

Excel, PDF, Word, even PNG screenshots — each carrier files differently, and some change format between filings.

GRIs caught too late

A missed increase or an expired contract costs thousands per shipment. By the time you notice, the margin is gone.

Quoting eats hours a day

Copy-pasting across spreadsheets to build a quote, every time. It is slow, error-prone, and delays the answer your customer is waiting for.
