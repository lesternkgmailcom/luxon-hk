Daily Market Snapshot

The Challenge

Regional management needs daily and weekly market rate summaries to make procurement decisions. But manually compiling rates from 13 carriers across 5 trade lanes took 2+ hours every day.

Different data sources — auto-extracted from databases plus manually entered from carriers without extractors — must be combined into a single coherent report. Coverage gaps, missing carriers, and stale data all had to be tracked manually.

The Solution

Automated daily snapshot

Pipeline pulls rates from SQLite databases and manual carrier config, validates coverage per trade lane (11 carriers for TPEB, 10 for WCSA, etc.), generates a formatted Excel report with 5 stacked trade sections, and auto-emails at 6 PM weekdays.

Smart coverage and validity logic

Forward-looking validity using today + 7 days. Per-carrier query patterns for complex routing. Backfill from direct DB queries when the snapshot drops a carrier.

Weekly executive report with AI review

AI composes an HTML email with rate tables, per-trade WoW analysis, regional narratives, and management-level commentary. A second AI agent reviews for content consistency, data accuracy, and tone before sending.
