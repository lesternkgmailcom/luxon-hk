Ocean Freight Rate Intelligence

How we built an AI-powered dashboard that automated rate tracking across 13 carriers on 5 major trade lanes.

The Challenge

Ocean freight rates change weekly across 13 carriers on 5 major trade lanes (Asia to US/Canada, Asia to Latin America, Asia to Europe). Keeping track of these movements is critical — a missed rate increase or expired contract can cost thousands of dollars per shipment.

But rate sheets arrive in wildly different formats: Excel (.xls and .xlsx), PDF, Word documents, Outlook .msg emails, inline email text, and even PNG screenshots of rate tables. Each carrier has a unique spreadsheet layout, and some change their format between filings.

The result: manual comparison took 4+ hours per day — copy-pasting into spreadsheets, prone to errors, with decisions delayed by days. Rate validity windows expired constantly, and GRI increases were caught too late.

The Solution

AI-powered extraction engine

44 carrier-specific extractor modules that read rate sheets in any format — Excel, PDF, Word, email, and PNG via vision AI — and extract structured data into normalized databases.

Comparison dashboard

Side-by-side rate comparison across all carriers, trade lanes, and destinations. Color-coded deltas show week-over-week changes at a glance.

Natural language queries

Ask "Shanghai to Manzanillo 20DC" and get instant rates with surcharges and validity information. Replaces complex multi-step spreadsheet lookups.

Daily market snapshot

Automated report generated and emailed every morning with current rates and week-over-week changes across all trade lanes.

Rate change detection

Color-coded increases and decreases, validity tracking, and GRI awareness. Catch rate movements before they impact negotiations.

Key Features

Rate comparison table

Side-by-side rates across all carriers with a week-over-week delta column. Color-coded increases (red) and decreases (green) make changes immediately visible.

Trade lane tabs

TPEB US, TPEB Canada, WCSA, ECSA, EUR — each with per-destination columns. Switch between trade lanes instantly to focus on what matters.

Carrier coverage tracking

13-carrier heatmap showing which carriers have fresh data, which are manual config, and which have gaps. Stale data alerts prevent decisions on outdated rates.
