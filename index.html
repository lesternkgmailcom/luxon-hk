Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our AI Workers and custom solutions.

What exactly is an AI Worker?
An AI Worker is an autonomous AI agent that runs 24/7, handling tasks like email triage, calendar management, WhatsApp responses, and document processing. It connects to your existing tools and acts on your behalf — like a digital employee that never sleeps. You don't need technical knowledge to use it; you interact through the channels you already use.
How is this different from ChatGPT or Copilot?
ChatGPT and Copilot are reactive — they respond when you ask. An AI Worker is proactive: it monitors your inbox, follows up with clients, drafts replies, and executes workflows without being prompted. It's built around your business processes, not general conversation.
Do I need technical knowledge to use this?
Not at all. We handle the entire setup — you don't write code or touch a terminal. After deployment, you interact with your AI Worker through email, WhatsApp, or Slack just like you would with a colleague. Our custom AI Solutions are designed with non-technical users in mind too, with intuitive dashboards and natural language interfaces.
How long does setup take?
AI Workers can be deployed same-day. After a brief discovery call, we integrate with your tools and have you live within 24 hours. Custom AI Solutions typically take 1–4 weeks depending on complexity, with a working prototype available for review early in the process.
Is my data safe?
Security is central to everything we build. AI Workers run in sandboxed environments with read-only permissions by default, firewall rules, and an instant kill switch to revoke any integration. Your data is completely isolated — we never sell it or use it to train AI models. Custom solutions are deployed on dedicated infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit.
What does a custom AI solution cost?
AI Solutions are project-based, priced after a discovery call where we understand your data sources, workflows, and goals. This allows us to scope the work accurately. AI Workers, on the other hand, have clear tiered packages starting from HK$3,500 — see our pricing page for details.
